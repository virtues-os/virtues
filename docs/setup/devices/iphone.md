The Virtues iPhone app needs iOS 26.2 or later.

## Pair your iPhone as your first device

> Not yet tested on real hardware.

1. Install the Virtues iPhone app from the App Store, and open it. Setup
   opens. Choose **Begin**.
2. At **Scan your server's code**, choose **Open the camera** and point it at
   the QR code the installer printed. The app pairs from the code alone.
3. If you can't scan it, choose **Find it on this network** or **Type the
   address and code**, enter the six-digit code the installer printed, and
   choose **Connect**.
   If the app says the code didn't work, it may have expired: run
   `virtues pair` on your server for a new one.
4. Name your assistant, say what it should call you, and choose your home.
5. At **What this iPhone can share**, choose **Turn on Location and Health**, and allow
   each one when iOS asks.
6. At **Choose how … thinks**, keep **A Virtues subscription** chosen,
   choose **Continue to checkout**, enter your email, open the link we send,
   and choose **Attach box**. Checkout then opens in your browser, where you confirm. If you
   already have an account, choose **I already have an account**. To use
   your own AI model, choose **Your own AI** and enter its address and key.
7. Choose **Open Virtues**. **A note from us** opens the founder's letter,
   which you can also read later from Setup in the app.

## Pair your iPhone from a paired computer

Open the Devices screen in the desktop app on your Mac or PC, and scan its QR
code with the iPhone app. This works even when your iPhone and server are on
different networks. Anyone who photographs that QR code gets access to your
server, so don't show it on a shared screen.
[Reaching your server](/docs/operate/reach#pairing-a-device) explains more.
After pairing, Setup opens **What this iPhone can share**.
